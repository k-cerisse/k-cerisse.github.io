### Serving for and with the youth - Couples for Christ Youth (CFC-Y)

**April 2022 - Present**

I first started out as a helper at the youth camps we hold twice a year. I was helping out by cleaning up after meals, praying for everyone during the duration of the weekend, and socializing with the attendees to create a welcoming environment. I was then asked for more services ranging from planning the logistics for the weekend and being part of the music ministry. Finally, in April 2023, I was able to lead the spring camp along with my male counterpart which we named "Revival". We discerned who would be part of the service team composed of facilitators, music ministry, assistants, session speakers, sharers, and prayer warriors. The attached photos are from the most recent fall camp we held in October 2025. I was asked to share a short part of my testimony at the camp and be a member of the music ministry.

The other photos are from that summer (2025) where I had the opportunity to attend many different conferences for CFC-Y, the young adults ministry: Singles for Christ (SFC), and even the kids ministry: Kids for Christ (KFC).
